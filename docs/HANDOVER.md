# Your website: how to update it

Everything on your site can be changed from your dashboard. Changes show up
on the site the next time a page is loaded. No need to ask anyone.

**Dashboard:** `https://<your-domain>/admin` (bookmark it on your phone).

## First sign-in

1. Sign in with the email and temporary password you were given.
2. Change it right away: **Profile → Password** (at the bottom). Changing it
   signs out any other phone or computer that was signed in.
3. Forgot it? Tap **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page and you'll get
   a reset link by email (valid for 1 hour, works once).
4. Too many wrong passwords in a row locks sign-in for a minute. Just wait.

## What each page does

| Page | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Change your name, the one-line tagline, the short intro, your About story, your profile photo, your timezone, and the private email where collab messages are sent. Press **Save changes**. |
| **Photos** | **Add photos** (pick several at once; big phone photos are shrunk automatically). Give each a caption and character/series, **Hide** ones you don't want shown yet, drag ⠿ or use ↑ ↓ to reorder, **Delete** to remove for good. |
| **Videos** | Paste a link from TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Twitch… and a title. YouTube gets its thumbnail automatically; for others you can **Add cover** with a screenshot. Visitors tap a card to watch it on that app. |
| **Socials** | Add your accounts. You can type `@yourname` instead of the full link. Your first Twitch, Kick, YouTube or TikTok link becomes the big **Watch** button at the top of your site. |
| **Schedule** | Add your regular weekly streams (day, start, end, what you're streaming). Under **Changes & extra streams**, add a **Day off** (with a note like "At AniCon!") or an **Extra stream**. Visitors see times in their own timezone automatically. |
| **Inbox** | Messages from your contact form. **Reply by email** opens your mail app. **Archive** files a message away; archived messages are deleted after a year. Messages also arrive in your email. |

## Good to know

- **Bold text in About:** wrap words in `**double stars**`. Leave an empty line
  between paragraphs. Links starting with `https://` become clickable.
- **Timezone:** set it once in Profile to where you stream from. Your schedule
  is entered in that timezone.
- **Nothing shows yet?** Sections with no content (no photos, no videos) stay
  hidden until you add something.
- **Something went wrong?** Take a screenshot of the message and send it to your
  developer. Errors are also reported to them automatically.
